This site is the starter that ships with Pillar. Everything on the home page —
the hero copy, the three cards, how many posts this list shows — is a setting
declared in a `<schema>` block, edited in the dashboard, and stored in
`templates/index.json`.

Open `pillar dev`, click the hero, change the heading. The file on disk changes
as you type, and `git status` sees it.
