---
title: "Pillar 0.1: files in, HTML out"
date: 2026-09-09T20:17
summary: A static site generator on Liqx, with a dev server whose dashboard drives real files, and a starter site.
tags: [release]
---
The first version: sites as folders of markdown, JSON and Liqx templates; sections whose `<schema>` block is their editing surface; layers for themes and addons; content-hashed, incremental builds. `pillar dev` serves a dashboard that edits the site's files directly, and the starter shows a landing page, a blog and docs.
