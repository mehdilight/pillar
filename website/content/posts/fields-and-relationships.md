title: A field builder, relationships and rules
date: 2026-09-10T21:55
summary: Content types are built in drawers from 29 field types — groups, repeaters, tables, galleries, files — with relationships between entries and rules checked on publish.
tags: [release, content]
---
The field builder picks types from a searchable grid and configures each field in a drawer of its own. New types: group, repeater, table, checkboxes, icon and file; images can be galleries, dates can carry a time. Entries link to entries, and templates read the entries themselves. Fields can be required, limited, conditional on other fields, or hidden — enforced when an entry is published, never on a draft. The dashboard's main bundle dropped from 605 KB to 237 KB on the way.
