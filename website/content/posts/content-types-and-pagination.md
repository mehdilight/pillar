---
title: Content types, pagination and a clickable canvas
date: 2026-09-09T20:34
summary: Collections get schemas and a form; templates paginate; sections are selected by clicking them in the preview.
tags: [content, editor]
---
Content types — `schemas/<collection>.json` — turn frontmatter into a form, created from the dashboard or `pillar make:collection`. Templates declare `paginate` and get `/blog/page/2/` and friends, with windowed page links. And the preview became a canvas: hover outlines a section, a click selects it.
